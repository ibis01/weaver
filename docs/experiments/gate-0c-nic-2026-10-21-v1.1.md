### Binding qualifications (verbatim; normative, not notes)

- Binding qualification Q1 (threshold provenance). - Binding qualification Q2 (non-sufficiency). Passing this floor establishes non-degeneracy only. An IQR of 0.10 on the 16-day grid corresponds to roughly **two distinct day-positions** of interquartile spread; **two day-positions do not establish scientific sufficiency** of the design, detectability of the primary effect, or any strategy property. Passing G2-P must not be cited as evidence of discriminative adequacy beyond the floor's literal content. Q1 and Q2 are binding text and must be reproduced verbatim in every results artifact's interpretation fields.
